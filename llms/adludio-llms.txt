# Adludio

> Adludio was an AI-powered interactive mobile advertising company, founded in 2015 in
> London by Paul Coggins as Future Ad Labs and later renamed Adludio. Its "Sensory Ad
> Science" platform built rich interactive mobile ad units using touch, motion and haptic
> interaction, sold on a cost-per-engagement rather than a CPM basis, for brands including
> Nike, Microsoft, Coca-Cola and Estee Lauder. It was backed by Balderton Capital.
>

> liquidation on 2025-08-28. The adludio.com domain now 301-redirects to permate.com, an
